# OCF Location Areas — Option B

**Geographic, by where you are on the ground.** The parents are the places you'd
stand in — named **neighborhoods** and booth **streets/lanes** — and the specific
spots (stages, landmarks, services) hang off whichever one they physically sit in.
This mirrors the map and how booth crews talk on the radio.

- **Every landmark has a home;** pick a broad area fast, or drill in.
- **Longer list,** and the one-level-of-nesting limit forces a few calls (see ⚠️).

Rows marked **(confirm)** were read off the map and need a local's confirmation.

> ⚠️ **One-level-nesting consequences:**
> - **White Bird** sits in the Main Stage meadow *and* is the parent of *Big Bird* —
>   it can't be both a child and a parent, so it stays its own top-level area.
> - A **street can't nest under a neighborhood** (that's two levels) — streets and
>   neighborhoods are siblings at the top.
> - **Camping** stays a flat parent of the camps, same as Option A.
